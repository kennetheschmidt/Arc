How Amplification Works

Amplification is a fifth tier beyond IV. It gives the gun its own upgrade tree, and every choice is permanent — some upgrades lock others out for good.
  1. Unlock the Outpost and expand it to level 4.
  2. Upgrade the Gunsmith to IV — 3 Radial Press, 3 Magnetic Accelerators and 1 Emperor Modulator, looted inside the fallen Emperor.
  3. Build the Research Station to level 4, which unlocks its Amplified Research tab.
  4. Get the weapon to tier IV, then Amplify it with an Amplification Module of its rarity. Durability goes 130 → 200.
  5. Research the upgrade, then buy it on the gun — each node costs materials of its own.

Up to five researches can run at once. Research points come from studying research items at the station, which consumes them. Looted amplified weapons arrive at about 10 durability of 200, so they are good for one fight. Amplified Fragments repair them — recycling a spare module gives 7. Stiletto and Bantam cannot be amplified.

Amplification Modules

Each tier unlocks amplification for one base-weapon rarity. Modules drop only from the Frigate and inside the fallen Emperor on Pendola Pass, and all five Marks share the same drop rate — a Mk. V is no rarer than a Mk. I.

Research Items
