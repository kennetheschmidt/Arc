How Amplification Works

Amplification turns a standard weapon into an Amplified variant.
  1. Complete a research task — each one studies a particular behaviour in the field.
  2. Obtain the Amplification Module matching the base weapon's rarity. A module only unlocks amplification for its own tier.
  3. Amplify the weapon to get the Amplified variant.

Amplification Modules

Each module tier unlocks amplification for one base-weapon rarity.
